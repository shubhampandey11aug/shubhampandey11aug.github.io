Workforce Intelligence

System Architecture Diagram

The Business Challenge

A technology consulting firm managed workforce reporting through manual SQL exports and Excel. With growth across three global hubs, this created 48-hour turnarounds and significant data quality issues (duplicates/invalid salaries).

Technical Implementation

Designed a Bronze-to-Silver lakehouse pipeline using a two-stage approach:

  • Bronze Stage: Incremental extraction from Azure SQL into a raw layer.
  • Silver Stage: PySpark transformation to deduplicate records and enrich employee data with department context.
  • Quarantine Logic: Implemented a separate path for invalid records to ensure only trusted data reached the reporting layer.

Governance

Established a strict reconciliation framework: Bronze (332 records) $\rightarrow$ Silver (307 records), with 20 quarantined and 5 duplicates dropped, ensuring 100% accountability.

Final Impact

Reduced report turnaround from 48 hours to near real-time, producing a trusted foundation for HR reporting.
